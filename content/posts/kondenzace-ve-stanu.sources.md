## Primary sources and scope

- NOAA, https://www.noaa.gov/jetstream/atmosphere/hydro — condensation, dew point, liquid water forming at a cooled surface. No unsupported numerical moisture production claims.
- MSR support, https://support.cascadedesigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/35376984957459-How-do-I-prevent-or-reduce-tent-condensation — breathing/skin, wet gear and environment; ventilation openings and separating fly from canopy.
- MSR tent manual, https://www.msrgear.com/on/demandware.static/-/Library-Sites-cdiSharedLibrary/default/dw576efedd/pdf/manuals/MSR_Tents10_OManual_EN.pdf — retrieved through indexed search; direct PDF retrieval redirects and fails. Confirms condensation can occur on the floor/sill, double-wall behavior and ventilation limits during humid weather. Do not treat a wet floor as proof of leaking.
- NEMO, https://www.nemoequipment.com/en-ca/blogs/journal/minimizing-and-preventing-condensation-in-your-nemo-tent — campsite, wind orientation by conditions, taut pitching, single-wall contact and gently wiping moisture. Do not repeat its altitude claims or a universal instruction to face the entrance into strong wind.
- Hilleberg, https://hilleberg.com/eng/products/accessories/footprints — abrasion protection and reducing ground moisture specifically when a footprint covers the vestibule. No claim that an extra sheet under a waterproof floor cures condensation.
- MSR support, https://support.cascadedesigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/35376957490067-How-do-I-keep-my-MSR-tent-clean-and-dry — moisture-related coating degradation, drying and cool/dry loose storage. No fixed guarantee for safe wet storage duration.
- NEMO, https://www.nemoequipment.com/blogs/journal/cleaning-repairing-and-storing-your-tent — completely dry components before storage; avoid inappropriate heat/machine drying. No universal cleaning chemicals prescribed.
- MSR, https://cascadedesigns.com/blogs/msr-gear-guides/tips-for-longer-tent-life — local seam leaks and material-appropriate repair; distinguish from condensation.
- CPSC, https://www.cpsc.gov/Safety-Education/Safety-Education-Centers/Carbon-Monoxide-Information-Center/Portable-Heaters--Camping-Equipment?language=en — combustion and carbon monoxide risk in enclosed camping shelters. MSR manual also prohibits fueled appliances inside or near tents. No vestibule-cooking exception given.

## Editorial decisions

- Clues to leakage are probabilistic; condensation and leakage can coexist.
- No promise that ventilation, two walls, a footprint or a high hydrostatic head eliminates condensation.
- Safe site and wind resistance take precedence over airflow; manufacturer instructions take precedence over generic setup changes.
- Morning checklist and the observation exercise are editorial practical guidance. Separating fly/inner depends on construction; wet packing is temporary, followed by drying.
- Each primary source contributes a short paraphrase; none is copied or closely translated. Source-derived details are spread across the sources above, with no more than 200 words attributed to any individual source.
