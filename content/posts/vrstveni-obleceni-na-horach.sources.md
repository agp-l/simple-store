## Primary and specialist sources

- BMC, [A guide to hill walking clothing and layers](https://www.thebmc.co.uk/en/a-guide-to-hill-walking-clothing-layers): layer functions, fleece, moisture and weather protection. The search retrieval included the substantive article; direct opening returned HTTP 403.
- BMC, [What to wear and what to take when hill walking](https://www.thebmc.co.uk/en/what-to-wear-what-to-take-when-hill-walking): flexible layers, waterproof trousers and side zips. Retrieved through search.
- Mountaineering Scotland, [Layering: what to wear for autumn in the Scottish hills](https://www.mountaineering.scot/news/layering-what-to-wear-for-autumn-in-the-scottish-hills): ventilation, condensation, changing layers, extra insulation for stops. Do not repeat its overly absolute claim that down becomes useless after absorbing any moisture.
- REI Expert Advice, [How to Choose Base Layers](https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/underwear.html): synthetic versus merino drying and odor, blends and fit. Avoid guarantees that any fabric always keeps the wearer dry.
- Mountain Equipment, [Layering Guide](https://www.mountain-equipment.com/pages/layering-guide): separate roles for walking insulation, shell and booster insulation. No brand technology or product recommendation copied.
- Mountain Equipment, [Synthetic Insulation Buying Guide](https://us.mountain-equipment.com/pages/synthetic-insulation-buying-guide): down versus synthetic weight, bulk, drying and damp performance. Neither is treated as inherently waterproof.
- GORE-TEX, [FAQ](https://www.gore-tex.com/en_uk/support/frequently-asked-questions): a saturated face fabric and impaired DWR do not by themselves prove a leaking membrane.
- GORE-TEX, [Outerwear Care Instructions](https://www.gore-tex.com/en_uk/support/care/outerwear): manufacturer-label-first care, no fabric softener, DWR restoration, exceptions for different garment constructions. No universal ironing or drying recipe given.
- HUDY, [Jak vrstvit oblečení na horách: průvodce pro ženy](https://www.hudy.cz/jak-vrstvit-obleceni-na-horach-pruvodce-pro-zeny): user-provided topic inspiration; fit, underwear, shell distinctions and timely changes cross-checked with sources above. Structure, wording and illustrative situations are original; expert quotes and gender generalizations are not reused.

## Editorial boundaries

- Three-layer terminology describes functions, not a compulsory count of garments.
- Weather scenarios are examples, not complete packing lists for winter or high-altitude trips.
- Wind resistance, waterproofness and insulation are kept distinct; softshell is not a uniform waterproof rating.
- Damp insulation still requires weather protection; there is no claim that wet down produces heat.
- Suggested practice, packing organization and the example day are editorial recommendations. They are not presented as firsthand trip experience.
- All links are included in the final article's sources block. No passages are quoted or closely translated; source-derived detail is distributed across the independent references above.
